BrainFood Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

BrainFood does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, emails, location, device identifiers, analytics, or any personal information.

Data Stored Locally On Your Device

The following is stored only on your device and never sent anywhere:

  • Your daily learning streak and best streak
  • Favourite cards you have saved
  • Cards viewing history
  • Pub Quiz session results and accuracy stats
  • Which quiz questions you have already answered
  • Word of the Day rotation

Uninstalling the app deletes all this data permanently.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device at the time you choose. No data is sent to any server. You can disable them in your device settings at any time.

Network Requests

BrainFood makes two outbound network requests, neither of which sends any personal data:

  • Version check: On launch, the app fetches a small JSON file from GitHub to check if a newer version is available on Google Play. No personal information is included in this request.
  • Over-the-air updates: The app checks Expo's update servers for bug-fix updates. No personal information is sent.

Third-Party Services

BrainFood uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Children

BrainFood is suitable for all ages. We collect no personal data from anyone.
