Fact Fight Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Fact Fight is designed with privacy in mind. The core app — facts, pub quiz, streaks, favourites — works entirely on your device and sends nothing personal anywhere. The optional Play vs Friend multiplayer feature does send limited data to Firebase (see below).

Data We Never Collect

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, location, device identifiers, advertising IDs, analytics, or any data that identifies you personally.

Data Stored Locally On Your Device

The following is stored only on your device and never sent anywhere:

  • Your daily learning streak and best streak
  • Favourite cards you have saved
  • Cards viewing history
  • Pub Quiz session results and accuracy stats
  • Which quiz questions you have already answered
  • Word of the Day rotation
  • Your chosen app theme (dark / light)
  • An anonymous random player ID used to identify you in multiplayer games (contains no personal information)

Uninstalling the app deletes all this data permanently.

Play vs Friend — Multiplayer Data

When you use the Play vs Friend feature, the following data is temporarily stored on Firebase (Google's servers) for the duration of the game:

  • The nickname you enter before the game
  • Your anonymous random player ID (generated locally, contains no personal information)
  • Your answers and score for that session
  • The room code and game state

This data is deleted from Firebase automatically when the game ends. It is not linked to any account, email address, or identity. The nickname you enter is the only user-provided text — choose any name you like.

Firebase is operated by Google. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy for Google's data practices.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device at the time you choose. No data is sent to any server. You can disable them in your device settings at any time.

Network Requests

Fact Fight makes the following outbound network requests:

  • Version check: On launch, the app fetches a small JSON file from GitHub to check if a newer version is available on Google Play. No personal information is included.
  • Over-the-air updates: The app checks Expo's update servers for bug-fix updates. No personal information is sent.
  • Multiplayer (Play vs Friend only): When you start or join a game, temporary game data is sent to and received from Firebase Realtime Database. This only occurs when you actively use the multiplayer feature.

Third-Party Services

Fact Fight uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only third-party service used is Firebase Realtime Database (Google), and only during active multiplayer sessions.

Children

Fact Fight is suitable for all ages. We collect no personal data from anyone.
